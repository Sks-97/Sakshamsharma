The shape of the trip

  • Fly into Mumbai (BOM) and out of Pune (PNQ). Open-jaw tickets avoid driving back to Mumbai, which would cost 3–4 hours.
  • One Mumbai hotel in Andheri East (airport / Saki Naka belt). SafeOne is 2–3 km away, Kalina is 7 km, the western highway goes north to Borivali and Vasai, and the airport is next door.
  • Lohar Chawl is the jackpot. Four customers (Lath warehouse, Maya, Ramesh, Hari Om) are within a 5–10 minute walk of each other in the Kalbadevi wholesale electrical market. Give it a full afternoon and walk it on foot.
  • Leave Mumbai through Dombivli. DBA Earthing (Nilje / Palava, Kalyan–Shil Road) sits on the way to the Taloja → Expressway route to Pune, so you visit it on the transfer day without doubling back.
  • Pune splits into a south loop and a north pair. Six customers sit in a 10 km loop (Sadashiv Peth → Dhankawadi → Katraj → Vadgaon Bk → Kirkatwadi → Karve Nagar). Akurdi and Alandi are 25–30 km north, but Alandi is 20 km from Pune airport, so it works as the last stop before your flight.
  • Before you book anything: four addresses need a phone call (Akshay, Voltterra, Fast Earthing, Veraizen), and one identity is doubtful (Global Tech). See section 2.

Section 3

Mumbai clusters

M-A · Andheri East / Kalina

SafeOne (Saki Naka) · Cabana (Kalina)

About 7 km apart, 25–40 min. Both are close to the airport and the hotel, so this is the arrival-day cluster.

M-B · Lohar Chawl / Kalbadevi

Lath (city office) · Maya · Ramesh · Hari Om

All within 300–600 m. Park once near Crawford Market and walk. About 20–25 km from Andheri East, 60–100 min depending on traffic. Market shops shut on Sundays.

M-C · Borivali / Vasai

Kihi (Borivali W) · Aryan (Borivali E) · Veraizen (Vasai E)

Borivali W to E is 3–4 km. Borivali to Waliv (Vasai E) is about 30 km on NH48, 50–70 min. From Andheri East it is 17 km to Borivali on the Western Express Highway.

M-D · Dombivli / Murbad

DBA (Nilje) · Lath factory (Murbad, optional)

DBA is about 40 km from Andheri (1.5–2 h) or 55–65 km from Vasai via Bhiwandi (1h45–2h30). It sits on the Shil Phata → Taloja → Expressway route to Pune. Murbad is a further 45 km (about 1h15).

Too far to combine: Lohar Chawl (south) and Vasai (north) are about 60 km and 2–3 hours apart. Never put them on the same day. Lath's Murbad factory does not fit into either itinerary unless you drop a Pune stop; meet Lath at Picket Cross Road instead.

Section 4

Pune clusters

P-A · South loop (6)

Global Tech (Sadashiv Peth) · Yash (Dhankawadi) · Fast (Katraj) · Voltterra (Vadgaon Bk) · Amit (Kirkatwadi) · Akshay (Karve Nagar or Ambegaon)

A clockwise loop of about 40 km: south down Satara Road, west along Sinhagad Road, and north to Karve Nagar. Each hop is 4–9 km, 15–35 min.

P-B · North (2)

Shreenath (Akurdi, PCMC) · Trimurti (Alandi)

Akurdi is about 22 km from Karve Nagar and 5 km off the Expressway at Ravet. Akurdi to Alandi is about 25 km, 50–70 min. Alandi to Pune airport is about 20 km, 40–60 min, which makes it a good last stop.

Pune timing: many shops in the old peths (Sadashiv Peth, Budhwar Peth) close for a long afternoon break, so the Sadashiv Peth visit is always scheduled in the morning. Ask every Pune customer which weekday they are closed.

Business meeting strategy

How to run the trip

Booking the appointments

  • T-10 days: call each company, ask for the proprietor or the person who buys copper and brass, and confirm the address (especially the five marked CHECK and Global Tech).
  • Follow up on WhatsApp with a one-page company profile PDF, your product list and the proposed time slot.
  • T-2 days: lock flights and hotels once at least 12 of 18 meetings are confirmed.
  • T-1 day: send a reminder message with your time window. On the day, message 30 minutes before you arrive.
  • Give windows ("between 11:00 and 11:30"), not exact times, especially in Mumbai.

If you want, I can draft the WhatsApp intro and reminder messages in your voice.

How many meetings a day

  • 4 driving meetings is the comfortable maximum in Mumbai; 6 only works when stops are walkable (Lohar Chawl) or in a tight loop (south Pune).
  • Plan 45 minutes per meeting and 75 minutes for factory visits (Veraizen, Lath Murbad).
  • Keep one buffer slot each day for overruns and walk-ins.

Who to prioritise

Ranked by likely metal consumption, based on what their listings say they make.

  • Tier 1, copper-bonded rod manufacturers: Veraizen, Lath Industries, Yash Earthing, Fast Earthing. They plate copper onto steel, so they buy copper anodes or cathode, plus brass for couplers and clamps. Give these the longest slots.
  • Tier 2, electrode and earthing-kit makers: Safeone, Cabana, DBA, Voltterra, Trimurti, Amit, Akshay, Shreenath. They buy copper plates, strips, flats and wire.
  • Tier 3, traders and dealers: Maya, Ramesh, Hari Om, Kihi, Aryan. Smaller tickets, but frequent repeat orders, and they know everyone in the market.
  • Unconfirmed: Global Tech. Visit only after the phone call.

Catalogue and samples

  • A 4–6 page catalogue: grades (ETP copper, EC-grade, brass alloys), forms and sizes, standards (IS / ASTM where applicable), MOQ, lead time, payment terms, delivery to Mumbai and Pune.
  • A price sheet linked to LME / MCX copper, so prices move with the published rate and there are no surprises.
  • Sample kit: 10–15 cm pieces of copper strip, flat, rod and wire, brass rod, and a copper anode offcut, each labelled with grade and size. Bring a mill test certificate copy for each.
  • Carry a soft copy on your phone to share on WhatsApp during the meeting.

How to pitch as a non-ferrous supplier

  • Open by asking what they buy today: copper grade, monthly tonnage, supplier, credit days, pain points (purity, delivery, price swings).
  • Lead with consistency: guaranteed purity with test certificates matters to rod makers, because poor copper causes bonding and plating failures in CPRI-type tests.
  • Offer a trial order at a small quantity, delivered to their door, to remove the risk of switching.
  • For traders: offer standard sizes ready in stock and fast dispatch from Delhi, or ask whether a Mumbai stock point would change their buying.
  • Close each meeting with one concrete next step: a quote within 48 hours, a sample test, or a trial order.

Final recommendation

Choose Option B, the 5-day plan. It costs about ₹25,000–30,000 more than Option A and gets you all 18 customers instead of 16. More importantly, each day has slack. In Option A, Day 2 chains four meetings with about 7 hours of driving through Bhiwandi and onto the Expressway, so a single delay in Mumbai pushes DBA late and lands you in Pune at night. First meetings are about building trust, and a rushed 45 minutes after a late arrival works against that.

Choose Option A only if you cannot be away for five days. In that case, handle Trimurti and Shreenath by video call and plan to see them on your Hyderabad / South India trip via Pune.
