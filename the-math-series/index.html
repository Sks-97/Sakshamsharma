What changed

Canva is one unit inside one cluster now

The last version took a single example and stretched it across thirty slots. This is the territory instead — you cut the series out of it.

The Canva tender lives in domain 03, cluster one, as one unit among four. It is an illustration of a mechanism, not a subject. Everything that was a “day” before is now a unit, and units are smaller than videos — several of them can share one script, or one of them can carry three.

The map is deliberately over-built. Two hundred-odd units is more than a year of daily posting, which is the point: a thirty-day run should be a selection with an argument behind it, and you should be able to draw a different thirty next quarter without rebuilding anything. Three example cuts are at the bottom.

Each domain is marked for how close it sits to your own practice — which tells you where you are teaching from the room and where you are translating someone else’s mechanics and need to check the figures.
